What these mockups are not

They are design references, not screenshots, not implementation, and not pixel-perfect runtime output. Runtime fonts, WebView2/egui rasterization, Windows 10 vs 11 DWM, and high-contrast system colors may differ. T3/T5/T7 must capture real screenshots and measurements in their evidence.
